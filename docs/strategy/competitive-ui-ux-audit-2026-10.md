## 2. How we show up — first-hand UI/UX read

### What is genuinely strong (keep, lean into)
- **An ownable, premium editorial identity.** Space Grotesk wordmark + large serif display headlines + dark theme + culture photography + amber accent. The homepage and mobile hero ("Who Captures Amapiano?") read like a real magazine — top-quartile against the entire comp set, and **best-in-class on mobile** (magazine-grade hero, clean deck, two clear CTAs).
- **The data-exhibit layer is a real moat.** In-house typed exhibits (bar / line / matrix / quadrant), the Bear Case module, and named-source attribution. Most competitors either embed third-party charts (Trapital → Chartmetric) or hide charts inside the PDF (MIDiA, BoF Insights). Our charts live in the reading experience. Very few do this.
- **Paid report pages convert cleanly.** The flagship (`whos-buying-african-sport-2026`) leads with a tight key-stat strip and a clear offer: **"BUY THIS REPORT — R3,500 · one-time · secure Paystack checkout · delivered as a PDF."** Price, terms and value are all above the fold. This surface is good.

### Where we leak (ranked by revenue impact)
1. **The storefront doesn't merchandise.** `/reports` cards show category + a "LIVE" badge + title — but **no price, no cover thumbnail, and no distinction between the 4 truly-paid PDFs and the 24 email-gated "premium" reports** (both read "MEMBERS / UNLOCK"). A scanning buyer cannot tell the R3,500 flagship from a free briefing. This is the literal conversion surface and it under-sells.
2. **No product → advisory bridge.** Every competitor that actually makes money routes the reader to high-ticket advisory or events — RockWater ("Work With Us" + case studies everywhere), Stears ("Request a Trial" + client-logo wall), MIDiA ("Speak to an analyst" on every report), BoF (Insights + VOICES), TechCabal (Moonshot + Cabal Creative + TC Insights). Our Culture-DD / Value-Capture advisory is the highest-margin product and it is **invisible on the money pages.** (Matches the standing outbound diagnosis: the $$$ product has no on-site conversion path.)
3. **The homepage over-indexes on breadth.** Too many competing CTAs (Read the story / Enter intelligence / Read the report / Read Signal / Open the desk / Explore the index / Take the checker / See the services…) and heavy insider nomenclature (Signal, Intelligence Desk, Source Desk, The Living Magazine, Signature Franchises, From the Desk). The **paid library is never surfaced up top.** Stratechery/Trapital show the opposite: one value line, one primary action, a clean format-sorted nav.
4. **Stale copy undercuts the depth.** `/reports` still says *"These editions are in development… the library becomes populated as verified research is completed"* — sitting directly next to **"56 EDITIONS LIVE."** A credibility ding that makes a full library look empty.
5. **Exhibits are static.** The Pudding is the global standard for data storytelling: scroll-triggered chart reveals + one interactive signature exhibit per piece. We already own the typed exhibits — animating them is the **highest-upside visual upgrade for the lowest content cost.**
6. **We're not using the African-audience distribution channel.** The Continent proves the pan-African pattern: a beautifully-typeset, portrait, forward-optimised **magazine PDF pushed through WhatsApp/email** (≈32k subscribers → ≈150k readership). We already produce PDFs; we don't distribute them where our readers actually share.

## 4. How we stack up — the verdict

- **Editorial design & data-viz craft: top quartile.** Genuinely competitive with Business of Fashion and Rest of World; ahead of TechCabal, Afridigest and Stears on visual richness; ahead of nearly everyone on *in-house* exhibits that live in the read (not just the PDF). Mobile is a standout.
- **Merchandising & conversion UX: bottom quartile.** The storefront, the paid/free legibility, and the product→advisory bridge are exactly where BoF Insights, MIDiA, RockWater and Stears are far ahead — and exactly where our external revenue (still R0) leaks.
- **Net:** we have a **Rest-of-World / BoF-grade front end bolted to a storefront that doesn't sell.** The craft is done. The commerce and conversion layer is the gap. This is consistent with the standing diagnosis: *the problem is conversion and advisory activation, not traffic or content.*

---

## 5. Prioritised fixes (conversion-first)

**A. Merchandise `/reports` (highest-impact, low effort).** Put **price + cover thumbnail + a clear PAID-PDF vs FREE/EMAIL chip** on every card; add a "Start here" trio (flagship + an entry report + a bundle); kill the stale "in development / commissioning slate" copy. Borrow MIDiA's category chips and BoF Insights' product-card merchandising.

**B. Add the advisory bridge (highest $ leverage).** A "Work with us / Speak to an analyst" block + a light trust strip on the report, bundle and `/reports` pages, routing to the Culture-DD / Value-Capture advisory. This is the on-site version of the outbound pipeline that was built but never fired.

**C. Tighten the homepage.** One value line, surface the shop above the fold, cut the CTA count to one primary + one secondary per section, and simplify the insider nomenclature. Steal Stratechery's focus and Trapital's format-sorted nav.

**D. Bring the exhibits to life.** Scroll-reveal the typed exhibits and build one interactive signature exhibit per flagship (e.g. a draggable Authorship→Capture quadrant). The Pudding standard, on assets we already own.

**E. Open the WhatsApp/email distribution channel.** A free, forward-optimised "issue" digest (or an Ownership-100 excerpt) pushed via WhatsApp/email as top-of-funnel — The Continent's channel, feeding our paid rail.

**Also worth copying regardless:** currency-localised pricing shown transparently (The Africa Report's ZAR line sits right at our R220 entry point); author/analyst hub pages (Puck); "Download PDF + assets" on paid reports (MIDiA); and events/convening as the high-margin third leg every serious player in this market runs (TechCabal, Semafor, BoF, Trapital).
