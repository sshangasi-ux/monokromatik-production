## Monthly Assessment — July 2026

---

### What the numbers say

The corpus has grown to 79 articles, but the median word count sits at 823 — less than half the 1,800-word floor the strategy demands. That gap is the loudest signal in the data: we are publishing frequently at the wrong depth. The Index holds 26 brands and 26 works, with only 2 snapshots taken since 23 June — not enough movement to generate a credible ranking narrative. Zero climbers and one newcomer in that window confirms the Index is largely static right now.

Case study quality is the structural problem underneath the volume problem. Average sources per case study is 2.2, average evidence pieces 2.3, and confirmed works sit at zero against 21 partial. Nothing has cleared the bar for a fully evidenced score. That means the Index's authority rests on partial work — which is fine as a build state, but it cannot be the permanent condition if we are positioning as the rating-agency standard.

On traffic, culture is the clear anchor (32 pageviews, up 16%), with direct and Vercel traffic both rising — suggesting the share-loop is beginning to function at a small scale. Music shows 11 pageviews with no category-level strategy yet visible. Drinks and sports are declining (−4% each) and entertainment is down 6%, which may simply reflect publishing volume rather than audience disinterest. Search has returned only 5 queries with zero opportunities surfaced — the SEO loop is not live in any meaningful way yet.

Performance scoring has reached 33 pieces, but 14 have been killed and zero have earned stars. That kill rate is healthy editorial discipline; the zero-star rate says we have not yet produced a breakout piece by our own scoring standard.
