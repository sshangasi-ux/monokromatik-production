# Monokromatik Editorial Voice Framework

## The core voice

**Cultural intimacy. Strategic sharpness. Commercial consequence. Distinct African judgment.**

Monokromatik should sound like a publication that is close enough to culture to understand what is happening, experienced enough in brand building to understand why it matters, and confident enough in Africa to treat the continent as a source of global influence rather than a recipient of global thinking.

## The editorial promise

Every substantial Monokromatik story should help the reader answer at least one of these questions:

- What is actually happening beneath the visible creative work?
- What does this reveal about audiences, identity, value or influence?
- Who authored or benefited from the brand move?
- What can African brand builders learn from it?
- What must global brands understand before attempting to participate?
- Where does the cultural moment create commercial, social or ownership consequence?

## Tone system

### We are

- Africa positive without becoming uncritical.
- Commercially literate without sounding like a consultancy brochure.
- Culturally alive without reducing culture to trend language.
- Bold in opinion while separating evidence from interpretation.
- Globally aware without seeking external validation for African value.
- Visually and verbally memorable without manufacturing hype.

### We are not

- Generic celebration media.
- Corporate marketing press-release language.
- Detached academic observation.
- Extractive cultural tourism.
- Performative provocation without evidence.
- A publication that describes Africa as one undifferentiated market.

## Writing cadence

A strong Monokromatik piece generally follows this rhythm:

1. **The opening tension** — a sharp observation, contradiction or human truth.
2. **The move** — what happened, by whom, where and in what context.
3. **The deeper read** — why the visible move matters culturally and strategically.
4. **The evidence** — credible sources, performance indicators, market signals or clearly identified limits.
5. **The consequence** — what brand builders, creators or investors should do with the insight.
6. **The lasting line** — a conclusion worth remembering or sharing.

## Signal voice by franchise

### The Work
Precise, forensic and fair. Celebrate great work only after explaining what made it intelligent.

### Will It Land?
Provocative, comparative and locally fluent. A strong global idea is not immune to African context.

### The African Advantage
Ambitious and authored. This is where thinkers make a case about Africa's role in future growth, culture and commerce.

### Culture Is Business
Commercially direct. Treat influence as value while respecting the people and communities that produce it.

### The Boardroom / The Backroom
Candid and useful. Avoid promotional interview fluff; pursue the decision, doubt, compromise and learning.

### Brand Weather
Sharp and economical. A weekly executive read: what moved, why it matters and what to watch next.

## The dual read: one story, two audiences

Monokromatik is a hybrid publication (see `MONOKROMATIK_2_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md` — Audience architecture). A substantial piece serves two readers from a single page:

1. **The cultural story** — the narrative, written for the everyday reader who came for the artist, the campaign or the moment. This is the body of the piece and follows the writing cadence above.
2. **The Brand Read** — a distinct, clearly separated module carrying the strategic consequence for the decision maker: who authored or benefited from the move, what brand builders should learn, and what global brands must understand before participating. It is the cadence's step 5 ("the consequence") made structural and visible.

The Brand Read must:
- be **led by a named point of view** — Sibu's take, or another leading marketer's or thought-leader's processed perspective, attributed clearly;
- separate evidence from interpretation, consistent with governance confidence status;
- never invent quotes, results or attributions (a processed perspective synthesises a real person's known, sourced positions — it does not fabricate a quotation);
- be optional per piece — a short cultural dispatch may carry no Brand Read; a flagship piece should.

The Brand Read is interpretive Signal-tier material. Under `EDITORIAL_GOVERNANCE.md` it requires human approval before publication today. The long-term intent is for the pipeline to draft it autonomously — leading with the operator's and invited thinkers' processed perspectives — within the same governance gate, not outside it.

## Contributor standard

External voices should be invited to contribute an argument, experience or decision insight—not generic biography or corporate promotion.

A contributor brief should ask:

- What is the single claim you are making?
- What have you seen or built that supports it?
- What should readers stop misunderstanding?
- What decision would you make differently now?
- How does African context change the conventional answer?

## AI generation and editing standard

Claude and OpenAI may assist with discovery, structure, research preparation, transcription, drafting and adaptation. They must be prompted against this framework and the editorial governance policy.

AI-generated or AI-assisted output must not:

- mimic the style of a named living person;
- invent sources, results or quotations;
- collapse African markets into generalisations;
- turn unsupported opinion into reported fact;
- erase authorship, attribution or contributor voice;
- publish high-consequence Signal or Intelligence material without required review.
