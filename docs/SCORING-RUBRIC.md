# Cultural-Signal Index — scoring rubric v2.0

**Effective:** 20 July 2026 · **Supersedes:** v1 (implicit, unwritten)
**Applies to:** all works scored for Edition 01 (baseline snapshot, 30 September 2026)

---

## Why this exists

Until now the Index defined its *mechanics* — four axes, levels 1–5, weighted
(IDEA .25 / AUTHORSHIP .35 / EXECUTION .15 / CONSEQUENCE .25), composite /100 — but
never defined **what a level actually means**. Levels were assigned by judgement with no
written standard, and judgement drifted generous.

The evidence, measured across 72 scored works:

| Band | Share |
|---|---|
| AAA (90–100) | 26% |
| AA (80–89) | 32% |
| A (70–79) | 21% |
| BBB (60–69) | 17% |
| BB (50–59) | 4% |
| B (40–49) | **0%** |
| C (<40) | **0%** |

**58% of all work scored 80+. Nothing has ever scored below 50.** A rating where most work
is "strong" and none fails is not a rating — it is praise with a number attached.

This was confirmed directly: when five thin case studies were rebuilt against their evidence
in July 2026, **CONSEQUENCE fell on five of five** (5→3 on three of them). The old scores had
rewarded *announcement*. The evidence rewarded *outcome*. That is a systematic error, not
five coincidences.

**Publishing this rubric is also the point.** Brand Finance, Interbrand, Kantar and YouGov all
publish a score; none publishes the band cut-offs or the level definitions behind it. We publish
both. That is the cheapest credibility available to an independent index.

## The axes

### IDEA — 25%
*How original and sharp is the strategic idea?*

| | |
|---|---|
| **5** | Genuinely new for the category. Nobody in this market had done it. Reframes what the category can attempt. |
| **4** | Strong idea, well adapted. Recognisable elsewhere, but new to this market or this brand. |
| **3** | Competent and conventional — the category's standard play, run knowingly and well. |
| **2** | Derivative. A borrowed idea with little adaptation to context. |
| **1** | No discernible idea. Activity without a thesis. |

### AUTHORSHIP — 35% *(the founding axis — weighted highest on purpose)*
*Who originated the work, and who controls it?*

| | |
|---|---|
| **5** | Conceived, made **and owned** by African/diaspora principals, including control of the IP, the mark or the rails. |
| **4** | African/diaspora-led with meaningful ownership, but a material layer — capital, platform, distribution — sits elsewhere. |
| **3** | Genuine African/diaspora participation in the making, but creative direction or ownership is external. |
| **2** | African/diaspora talent or aesthetics used as **input**. Authorship and ownership are external. |
| **1** | African culture referenced or extracted with no African authorship at all. |

### EXECUTION — 15%
*How well is the work actually made?*

| | |
|---|---|
| **5** | Flawless and coherent across every surface. Sets the standard others copy. |
| **4** | Strong and consistent; minor gaps that don't undercut the whole. |
| **3** | Solid where it counts, uneven elsewhere. |
| **2** | Visible weaknesses that undercut the idea. |
| **1** | Poorly made. The execution defeats the idea. |

### CONSEQUENCE — 25% *(the axis that was being abused)*
*What measurably changed — and who captured the value?*

| | |
|---|---|
| **5** | A durable, **third-party-verifiable** outcome, with value demonstrably captured by the African/diaspora party. Audited or published figures, sustained position, or structural change that has persisted **12 months or more**. |
| **4** | A real, documented outcome — but durability is unproven **or** capture is partial/shared. |
| **3** | Plausible impact with no published evidence; **or** impact that accrued mainly to a non-African counterparty. |
| **2** | Little discernible consequence. The moment passed. |
| **1** | Negative consequence, or value demonstrably surrendered. |

> ### The announcement rule
> **A launch, a signing, a partnership reveal or a stated intention scores CONSEQUENCE 3 at
> most — however large the brand or loud the coverage.** Consequence is what happened *after*.
> If the only evidence is that the thing was announced, it is a 3.
>
> A score of **5 requires evidence a third party could check.** Our own reporting is not
> evidence of consequence.

> ### Don't score what hasn't happened
> **A work that has not yet taken place is not scored at all.** No decode, no composite —
> `decode: []` plus a `scoreWithheld` line stating why, published in place of the matrix.
>
> Rating a forecast is a category error, and **a low score is the same error as a high one**:
> both assert a verdict on something with no result to verify. Two works in the v1 corpus were
> scored months before the event they described (AfroPlus Fest, September 2026; Dakar 2026),
> one of them at 80/100 — including EXECUTION 4 for an event nobody had seen.
>
> Withholding is not a gap in the Index. It is the Index declining to guess, and it is stated
> on the page. Score it after the event, on what it actually did.

## Scoring rules

1. **Score the work, not the brand.** A great company can make a 2.
2. **Evidence or drop a level.** If a level can't be justified from `evidence.confirmed`,
   it is one level too high.
3. **`reported` doesn't earn a 5.** Anything resting on `evidence.reported` caps CONSEQUENCE at 4.
4. **Use the whole scale.** 1s and 2s are valid and expected. A distribution with no failures
   is a broken instrument.
5. **Recency cuts both ways.** New work can score high on IDEA and AUTHORSHIP; it rarely earns
   CONSEQUENCE 5, because durability takes time.
6. **Re-score on new evidence.** Scores are versioned, not permanent. Movement is the product.

## Bands

| Band | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **AAA** | 90–100 | Exceptional — authored, executed and consequential |
| **AA** | 80–89 | Strong — clear authorship, real consequence |
| **A** | 70–79 | Sound — good work, incomplete capture |
| **BBB** | 60–69 | Mixed — the idea outruns the ownership |
| **BB** | 50–59 | Weak — visibility without value capture |
| **B** | 40–49 | Poor — borrowed authorship, thin consequence |
| **C** | <40 | Failing — value created, value surrendered |

Cut-offs are fixed. Changing them is a methodology change and must be dated here.

---

## Versioning & corrections

- Every score carries the rubric version it was set under (`v2.0` from 20 July 2026).
- Re-scoring under a new rubric is a **methodology change**, disclosed — not a silent edit.
- Any brand may challenge a score with evidence; corrections are published, not quietly applied.
- **Expected effect of v2.0:** scores fall, particularly on CONSEQUENCE. That is the correction
  working, not a downgrade of the work.
