## The offer in one line
**License the right to display your Cultural-Signal Index rating** — a verifiable "Ranked by MonoKromatik" badge, embeddable on your site, deck, case-study page or campaign, backed by the published, evidence-led Index.

## Why a brand wants it
- **Third-party validation of creative + cultural work.** Not self-praise — an independent, authorship-weighted rating from a named analyst, with the evidence public.
- **A number to point to.** "Ranked #— on the Cultural-Signal Index" is the kind of proof point that wins pitches, board slides and award entries.
- **Live, not static.** The badge reflects the current score and rank; as the brand's body of work grows, the rating can climb — and the badge moves with it.
- **Scarce.** Only rated brands can license it, and the rating is earned on the work, not bought.

## What's included
- The embeddable **"Ranked by MonoKromatik" badge** (score + rank + evidence tier), served live from `/api/badge/[brand]`.
- A **share-card** asset (1200×630) for social and decks.
- A link to the brand's **public Index page** (score, four-axis breakdown, Evidence Strength, movement).
- **Right to display** the rating in marketing, pitches and award submissions for the licence term.
- A **methodology + corrections** reference, so the number is defensible when challenged.

## What it is *not*
A paid rating. **The score is set independently by the analyst against the evidence and is never for sale** — licensing only grants the right to *display* it. If the work doesn't rate, no cheque changes that. That's the entire basis of its credibility.
