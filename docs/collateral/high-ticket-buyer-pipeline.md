# High-Ticket Buyer Pipeline — Culture DD & Value-Capture Advisory

*Built 2026-09-29. The revenue-outbound motion, aimed at the buyers who actually pay: investors and rights-holders — not the rated brands (that's the [badge/backlink play](brand-outreach-batch-01.md), an SEO-authority flywheel, not a revenue lever).*

> **The one rule that makes this different from the badge batch:** this is not a blast and it is not a flatter-the-subject play. It is 1:1, evidence-first, and it opens on **a real deal the buyer is actually working on** — not on their vanity score. Every send is logged in [`outbound-tracker.csv`](outbound-tracker.csv) and followed up, or it doesn't count.

---

## 1 · Two buyers, two SKUs

| SKU | Buyer | The decision it answers | Band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Culture Due Diligence** | PE / VC / growth equity / DFIs / corp-dev / sovereign capital buying **into** an African brand, culture, sport, music or fintech asset | *"Should we buy in, at what value, and what's the authorship & reputational risk?"* | Bespoke — on application (position as $8k–25k+) |
| **Value-Capture Advisory** | Rights-holders, IP & catalogue owners, federations, labels, studios, talent-holdcos | *"How do we own the certification apparatus and stop the value leaking offshore?"* | from **$5,000** |
| *(bridge)* **Signal Fit** | Brands/agencies choosing a partnership | *"Which property/talent actually fits, on signal not reach?"* | from **$1,500** — the smaller yes that warms a cold account |

The engine behind all three is the same: the Cultural-Signal Index + the AOC framework (Authorship → Ownership → Capture) + the signalling lens. Outbound sells the **decision**, never "a report."

## 2 · The Culture-DD partner dependency — sequence around it, don't stall on it

Culture DD is delivered **with a corporate-finance / legal partner** (per `lib/commerce.ts`), and that partner is **not yet signed**. Do **not** let that block outreach. The sequence books **discovery calls**, which need no partner. The partner is only engaged at **proposal/scope stage**, by which point there's a live opportunity to bring them into. So:

- **Now:** run the full Culture-DD sequence → book discovery calls → qualify → scope.
- **In parallel (do this week):** line up 1–2 corp-finance/legal partners so that when a discovery call converts, the proposal can name a delivery team. *(This is itself a Segment-C conversation — see the target list; a boutique that refers deals can also be the delivery partner.)*
- **Never** promise a delivery date on a first call until the partner is confirmed. Sell the *read and the framework* (which is yours alone) on the call; scope the DD after.

## 6 · Cadence & rules (so it actually runs)

- **Volume:** 5 new HOT targets/week, fully personalised. Quality over volume — 5 researched beats 50 blasted.
- **Follow-up is non-negotiable:** email 2 at +5 working days, email 3 at +7. ~80% of replies come after touch 1. No tracker row = it didn't happen.
- **Multi-touch:** LinkedIn connect + a genuine comment on their post the same week as the email. Warm the name.
- **Dormant nurture:** a "no / not now" is a **future yes** — tag `nurture` in the tracker, add them to the Ownership Brief list, re-touch when a relevant report ships or a deal in their space breaks.
- **Never:** blast/BCC, attach a deck cold, propose blind, promise a Culture-DD date before the partner is confirmed, or fabricate a person/email (route via role + LinkedIn / comms@).
- **The first close is the unlock:** landing even one Value-Capture or Culture-DD engagement is the proof point (a named client + a case) that makes every subsequent send warmer.
