# Brand-outreach kit — Batch 01 (August 2026)

*The turnkey batch for the backlink/badge outreach (see `docs/ops/seo-backlink-plan.md`). Each brand below is a founder-led / challenger African brand that scored well on the Cultural-Signal Index — the ones most likely to actually display or share a rating. Every embedded badge is a backlink (fixes the discovery problem) and every reply is a warm door to a paying relationship.*

## How to use (≈10 minutes)

1. Pick a brand card below.
2. Copy the **base email** once into your mail client; per send, swap in that brand's **subject**, **hook paragraph**, **score link** and **badge embed**.
3. Send from your own address (`editor@monokromatik.com`). Personal, not a blast — BCC nobody.
4. Log who you sent to and when. Re-check **GSC → Links** in ~2 weeks to see which embeds landed.

**Order to work through:** the fashion/design founders (Magugu, Iamisigo, Tongoro, Maison Château Rouge, MaXhosa, Boyedoe, Bathu) tend to reply fastest and care most about prestige recognition. The fintechs (Paystack, Moniepoint, Carry1st) have PR teams — route via press/comms. Chicken Licken is best reached via its agency, Joe Public. Kava via Inkblot/Filmhouse.

## Why this doubles as the revenue wedge

The ask on the surface is a badge. The real move is starting a relationship with a rated brand from a position of having done unpaid, credible work about them. A brand that embeds the badge is telling its own audience it trusts the rating — which is the exact posture that makes a paid Index/analysis relationship, an Edition 01 mention, or a licensing conversation land later. Backlinks are the near-term win; the warm door is the compounding one.
