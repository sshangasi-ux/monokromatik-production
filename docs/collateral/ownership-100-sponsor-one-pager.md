## The asset
**The Ownership 100** is the definitive ranked ledger of who owns Africa's most valuable brands — the phone in your hand, the channel on your screen, the drink in your glass — scored on the Authorship → Ownership → Capture lens, every entry sourced. It's the citable reference for the single question the continent keeps asking: *who actually owns this, and where does the value land?*

It is built to be the **annual tentpole** — the piece that gets cited, shared and returned to, and the one a sponsor wants their name on.

## Why it's worth presenting
- **The authority position.** Not an ad next to content — your brand *presents* the reference work on African ownership. The credibility rubs off because the work is independent and sourced.
- **A decision-maker audience.** The readership is brand, agency, investor and rights-holder leaders across Africa and the diaspora — the people who move budgets and deals.
- **Compounding reach.** A living, machine-readable asset (JSON/CSV at `/api/index`, Dataset schema for AI engines) that earns citations and backlinks all year — your sponsorship rides that, not a one-week flight.
- **On the right side of the story.** The whole franchise is about keeping African value in African hands. A sponsor seen backing that is making a statement, not buying an impression.

## What a presenting sponsor gets
- **"Presented by [Brand]"** on the live `/ownership-100` page (header slot) and the print/PDF edition, clearly disclosed.
- A **one-line brand message** beneath the credit.

- A mention in the launch dispatch to the newsletter list.
- *(Optional, by arrangement)* a co-branded data cut or a private briefing on the edition's findings.

Sponsorship is **native and clearly labelled** ("SPONSORED · PRESENTED BY …"). It **buys presentation, never the rankings** — the methodology is public and the scores can't be bought. That independence is exactly what makes the slot worth having; we protect it.
