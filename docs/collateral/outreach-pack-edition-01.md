# Badge outreach pack — Edition 01 (ready to send)

*Turns `docs/collateral/brand-outreach-templates.md` (Template A) + the Tier-1 target list in
`docs/ops/seo-backlink-plan.md` into **personalised, ready-to-send emails** — the last mile
between the plan and actual backlinks (the #1 SEO lever). Numbers are pulled live from the
Index (rank is out of 80 scored brands).*

**How to use (works from the iPad, in Gmail):**
1. Pick a brand below → copy the subject + body into a new Gmail.
2. Fill the two ‹brackets›: `‹first name›` and, if you have it, a specific `‹call link›`.
3. Find the recipient: the brand's **founder / head of marketing / brand lead** — LinkedIn or the
   brand's "Contact / Press" page. Founder-led brands (most of these) often list a direct address.
4. Send. If they bite → follow with **Template B** (the $900 Scorecard upsell) from the templates doc.
5. Log who you sent to; re-check **GSC → Links** every 2–3 weeks to watch links land.

> The big three — **Ethiopian Airlines, Capitec, Checkers Sixty60 (all 95)** — are slow
> multinationals; send them `brand-notification-edition-01.md` for completeness, not a badge pitch.
