## POST A — Loeries 2026  (company page or personal)

South Africa just made some of the best advertising on earth.

The harder question is the one the trophies never answer: who keeps the value?

At the 2026 Loeries, four pieces took the Grand Prix, and Joe Public swept both agency titles
with ~30 Loeries — an independent beating the networks on their own scoreboard. The craft is
world-class and unmistakably local.

But follow the brands behind the metal. Bar de Change for Heineken. African Time for AB InBev.
Spinning Thing for Unilever. Brand of the Year went to Heineken Beverages. Three of the four
most-celebrated ideas of the year were built to make foreign-owned brands stronger.

A trophy measures authorship — who made the idea. It says nothing about ownership — who keeps
the value the idea creates. A brilliant year for South African craft and a thin year for owning
it can be the same year.

## POST B — Cannes Lions 2026 × Africa & the diaspora  (company page or personal)

Kenya just won its first-ever Cannes Lions Grand Prix.

It was the only Grand Prix awarded to the entire Middle East & Africa region all week.

"Paid Sick Leave for Cows" — a local brand, a local agency, a globally legible idea. On our
read, it's the cleanest African win to actually own, because the value has the best chance of
staying in Kenya's own creative economy.

Nigeria took a rare Gold with "The Real Nigerian Prince" — but the credit is split across
Lagos, Singapore and London, and a shared win is a shared asset. The biggest share tends to
flow to the strongest network.

The pattern across the continent's haul: Africa's most open door at Cannes is the social-impact
lane, and the diaspora is the amplifier, not the audience. The whole region took 27 Lions.
Ogilvy alone took 81.

After the applause, one question decides it: whose balance sheet is heavier?
