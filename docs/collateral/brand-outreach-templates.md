# Brand-Outreach Templates — "You were rated"

*The activation collateral. The Index only matters when the rated brands know their score and act on it. These are paste-and-personalise templates. Fill the `[[brackets]]` from the brand's Index page (`/intelligence/signal-index/[brand]`).*

---

>

And if you think we got anything wrong, you have a right of reply — send evidence and it's reviewed on the same methodology.
>
> Worth a short call?
>
> — [[Sibu]]

### Sequencing note
1. Send **A** to the ranked brand. 2. If interested → **B** (scorecard) and/or the badge one-pager. 3. If they dispute → **C** (turns friction into engagement + a reason to return). 4. Run **D** in parallel to earn citation — one outlet writing *"per the MonoKromatik Cultural-Signal Index…"* compounds the authority faster than more content does.
