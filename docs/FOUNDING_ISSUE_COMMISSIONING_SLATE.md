# Monokromatik Founding Issue Commissioning Slate

## Issue 001 working title

# The Intelligence Behind African Influence

## Purpose

The founding issue must prove three things at once:

1. Monokromatik can interpret cultural and creative influence with a distinctly African strategic lens.
2. Signal is an authored editorial platform, not an aggregation feed.
3. Intelligence can translate stories and campaign evidence into reusable professional insight.

## Content standard

Every founding issue feature should be capable of becoming:

- a premium visual web story;
- a Weekly Signal newsletter feature;
- a structured Intelligence record;
- a shareable social derivative;
- a designed page or spread in a future downloadable/print edition.

## Initial contributor categories

The first outreach list should include credible voices across:

- African CMOs and regional brand leaders;
- African creative directors, strategists and agency founders;
- sport, music, fashion, hospitality and entertainment commercial operators;
- diaspora founders and brand/community builders;
- cultural researchers and effectiveness specialists;
- creators who can speak to authorship, ownership and partnership quality.

Contributors should be invited for a sharp point of view, first-hand case insight or informed challenge—not generic profile content.

## Commissioning questions

### For marketers and brand leaders

- Which decision materially changed the outcome of the work?
- What did local context tell you that global assumptions could not?
- Where did the brand invest beyond visibility into trust or participation?
- What commercial consequence can be supported by evidence?

### For creators and cultural operators

- Where did authorship sit—and who captured the value?
- What would meaningful brand participation look like in this space?
- What did the audience recognise that an outside observer might miss?

### For agency and strategic leaders

- What was the original tension or problem?
- What creative choice created relevance?
- What failed, was compromised or should be challenged next time?

## Media and design requirements

The founding issue must not be text-first. It requires:

- one signature cover visual treatment;
- one high-impact hero motion/video asset or official embedded campaign film;
- credited campaign imagery for The Work;
- portrait or filmed contributor assets for Conversations;
- designed Intelligence record pages;
- a visual Brand Weather modular system;
- downloadable issue-cover and social-card assets.

Media sourcing must follow the attribution-led policy in `docs/SOURCE_AND_RIGHTS_POLICY.md`.

## Publication and approval standard

| Content type | Research status | Human approval |
|---|---|---|
| Founding manifesto | Source-supported argument | Required before publication |
| The Work case study | Verified source pack and media classification | Required |
| Will It Land? analysis | Source pack, contextual reasoning and claim review | Required |
| Brand Weather | Attributed source signals | Review during launch phase |
| Contributor conversation | Consent, disclosures and quotation approval | Required |
| Intelligence record | Source ledger, metadata and confidence rating | Required before public or paid product use |

1. Select the specific campaign/work for the first **The Work** analysis.

3. Identify three to five founding contributor voices.
4. Build research packs and media/rights classifications for each selected feature.
5. Art-direct the Issue 001 cover and hero motion asset.
6. Publish the first linked Signal and Intelligence records through a controlled approval workflow.
