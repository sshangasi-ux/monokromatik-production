# Editorial Governance

## Public trust promise

**AI-assisted discovery. Human-directed intelligence. Verified sourcing.**

## Content risk tiers

### Tier 1 — Operational automation
May run automatically:
- source monitoring;
- metadata collection;
- deduplication;
- topic classification;
- related-content recommendations;
- internal dashboards.

### Tier 2 — Draft autonomously, publish through controlled rules
- culture briefs;
- entertainment and sport signal summaries;
- newsletter drafts;
- social adaptations of approved editorial.

### Tier 3 — Human approval mandatory
- Campaign Decoded;
- Would It Travel?;
- Market Lens reports;
- Diaspora Demand reports;
- premium intelligence;
- commercial-performance claims;
- critical brand analysis;
- sponsored content;
- corrections and disputes;
- sensitive cultural or reputational matters.

## Every Signal story must display or store

- factual source ledger;
- verified/partial/interpretive confidence status;
- publication and last-updated dates;
- brand, market and category metadata;
- clear fact-versus-analysis separation;
- rights status for visual assets;
- human approval status;
- sponsorship disclosure where applicable.

## Corrections standard

Errors must be corrected visibly with:
- correction date;
- changed statement;
- reason for correction;
- retained source record.

## Current production risk to remove

The current automated Editor-in-Chief workflow can fail open. That is acceptable only as a historical implementation note; no Signal or premium intelligence content may be governed by fail-open publication.
