## 1 · The brand

**Name:** MonoKromatik (one word; camel-cap **K** on *Kromatik*). Full mark: **MonoKromatik Network**.

**What it is:** An African & diaspora **brand-intelligence editorial network** — a rating agency for African cultural and commercial work.

**The essence:** *Monochrome discipline, one signal of colour.* The name is the brand: a black-and-white intelligence lens with a single, decisive accent — the amber "signal." Everything is rigour and restraint, punctuated by one point of heat.

**Positioning line:** African & diaspora brand-intelligence — home of the authorship-weighted **Cultural-Signal Index**.

**The wedge:** *Who authored the culture, and who captured the value?* — asked with the rigour of a research desk, never the credulity of a fan account.

**Personality:** Authoritative · evidence-led · culturally fluent · decisive · unhyped. Think *Monocle* / *Business of Fashion* / a ratings agency — for Africa's creative economy.

**Values:** Authorship over imitation · Evidence over opinion · Provenance as product · Judgement with a name.

## 2 · Logo & wordmark

**Primary wordmark:** `MONOKROMATIK` set in the display face (Space Grotesk), bold, uppercase, with **"KROMATIK" in amber** and **"MONO" in ink/white**. The optional descriptor `NETWORK` follows, small, in grey, letter-spaced.

- **Construction:** MONO (ink or white) + KROMATIK (amber `#CC5500`) + NETWORK (grey `#666666`, ~40% of the wordmark size, tracking +0.2em).
- **Clear space:** keep clear space of at least the cap-height of the "M" on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 90px wide on screen / 22mm in print (drop "NETWORK" below this).
- **Monogram / avatar:** a solid ink square with an amber **"MK"** or the analyst monogram **"SS"** (used on the methodology page) in Space Grotesk bold — for favicons, social avatars, stamps.

**Misuse — never:** recolour "KROMATIK" to anything but the amber; add gradients, shadows or outlines; stretch/skew; place on a busy photo without a solid scrim; use the amber on the whole wordmark; re-space the letters.

## 4 · Typography

A three-face editorial system (self-hosted). The serif carries emotion; the grotesk carries authority; the sans carries the reading.

| Role | Typeface | Use | Weights |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** | **Space Grotesk** | Wordmark, headlines, labels, UI, data figures, buttons | Medium 500, Bold 700 |
| **Feature serif** | **Fraunces** | Standfirsts, pull-quotes, feature headlines, editorial moments | Regular / Italic, Semibold |
| **Body** | **Inter** | Long-form reading, captions, fine print | Regular 400, Medium 500 |

- **Fallbacks:** Space Grotesk → Arial/Helvetica; Fraunces → Georgia/Times; Inter → system-ui/Arial.
- **Labels / eyebrows:** Space Grotesk **bold, uppercase, tracking +0.2–0.35em**, small (10–12px) — the signature "intelligence label" (e.g. `THE CULTURAL-SIGNAL INDEX`). This tracked-uppercase micro-label is a core brand device.
- **Headlines:** Space Grotesk bold, tight leading (0.95–1.0), large. Feature headlines may switch to Fraunces italic for warmth.
- **Standfirst / dek:** Fraunces italic, generous size — the editorial "voice-in" under a headline.
- **Body:** Inter, ~18px, 1.6 leading, comfortable measure (60–75 chars).
- **Numbers:** always tabular (lining) figures — scores, rankings, stats read as data.

## 5 · Voice & tone

**We sound like:** a senior analyst who respects the reader — precise, declarative, African/diaspora-centred, business-literate. Evidence first, opinion labelled as opinion.

- **Do:** lead with the specific and the sourced; separate fact from interpretation ("MonoKromatik's read:"); be decisive; write short, load-bearing sentences; centre authorship and value-capture.
- **Don't:** hype, PR-speak, tabloid net-worth figures, hollow superlatives, fan-account gush, hedging.
- **Signature moves:** the dual read (what happened → what it means); the authorship question; the honest downside; the named byline on every judgement.
- **Tagline bank:** *Who authored the influence.* · *The signal, weighted for authorship.* · *A rating agency for African cultural-brand work.* · *Provenance as product.*

## 6 · Design language

- **Grid:** editorial, generous margins, strong left alignment, clear hierarchy. Restraint over decoration.
- **Rules & hairlines:** thin `#E5E5E5` rules and amber underlines segment content — very *Monocle*.
- **Data-viz house style:** monochrome + a single amber fill; value labels always present (never colour alone); the **Cultural-Signal scorecard** (weighted axis bars + composite /100 on ink) is the flagship graphic device.
- **Imagery:** documentary, high-contrast, culturally specific; credit every source; when text sits on a photo, use a solid ink scrim. Prefer real, attributed stills over stock.
- **Motion (digital):** minimal, purposeful — fades and reveals, no gratuitous animation.
