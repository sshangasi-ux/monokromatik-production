## Lead ask — read this first (capacity gate)

Culture Due Diligence (CDD) markets as *"delivered with a corporate-finance/legal partner."* As of the last check that partner was **not yet signed**. So the first wave **leads with what is deliverable today off the engine + founder**, and holds CDD as the by-selective-engagement anchor:

- **Primary offer in the opener → Signal Scorecard + Value-Capture Advisory** (a sharp, self-contained value-capture/authorship read on a named asset — deliverable now, priced accessibly, proof-rich via the published reports).
- **Anchor / upsell → Culture Due Diligence** (the full deal-grade AOC read). Positioned as "by selective engagement" so demand is qualified *before* we commit delivery. Only hard-sell CDD once the CF/legal partner is signed — **confirm that status before the wave goes out.**

The service exists to answer one question a bank or Big-Four deal team doesn't: *you can buy the brand — did you buy the value?* Authorship → Ownership → Capture (AOC). Enquiry path: `/work-with-us?interest=culture-dd`.

## The sequence (3 touches + break-up) — draft copy

Tone: peer-to-peer, insight-first, zero fluff. One asset per send. Every send carries a **named, specific** value-capture observation about *their* space — never a generic pitch. Personalise the bracketed bits per target.

**Touch 0 — LinkedIn connect (no pitch).**
> Hi [name] — I write MonoKromatik, the value-capture intelligence desk on African brands, sport, music and fintech. Your work on [their deal/space] is squarely in what we map. Following with interest.

**Touch 1 — Email, day 0 (the insight, soft).** Subject: *[Their space] — who's actually capturing the value?*
> [Name] — quick one from MonoKromatik, where we do the value-capture read on African culture assets.
>
> On [their asset/category]: the financials price [X], but the question that moves the return is who *authored* the value and who owns the apparatus that prices it — because that's what decides whether the asset keeps the upside after the deal. We just published the read on [most-relevant report] — [one specific finding].
>
> If a value-capture + authorship read would sharpen a live thesis, I can send our Signal Scorecard on a named asset. Worth a look? → [report link]

**Touch 2 — Email, day 4 (proof + the anchor).** Subject: *The diligence the bank doesn't run*
> [Name] — the piece we run alongside the numbers: Authorship → Ownership → Capture. "Who's Buying African Sport?" is the worked example — a named deal ledger, a value-leakage model, scenarios to 2035. [link]
>
> Two ways we work: a **Value-Capture Scorecard/Advisory** on a specific asset (fast, self-contained), or full **Culture Due Diligence** on a live deal (by selective engagement). Happy to scope either against something you're looking at.

**Touch 3 — Break-up, day 10.** Subject: *Closing the loop*
> [Name] — I'll leave it here so I'm not clogging your inbox. If an African culture/brand/sport/fintech asset crosses your desk where "did we buy the value?" is the open question, we're the desk for that read. Reports are free to browse → [/reports]. All the best — Sibu

Minimum viable tracker (a sheet or the CRM): `firm · segment · contact · role · verified? · touch-0/1/2/3 dates · reply · status · next-action`. A scaffold lives at `docs/outbound/tracker.csv`. Work it in small weekly batches (5–8 verified contacts), not one big blast.

**Before any send:** (1) confirm the CF/legal partner status to decide how hard to push CDD vs. Scorecard/Advisory; (2) verify each contact is current; (3) Sibu approves the batch. Nothing auto-sends.
