# Source and Rights Policy

## Source hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Primary sources
Official brand, agency, campaign, athlete, creator, event, company, sport-property or institutional materials.

### Tier 2 — Authoritative professional sources
Reputable marketing, advertising, research, business, awards and effectiveness platforms.

### Tier 3 — Cultural-context sources
Credible African and diaspora culture, entertainment, fashion and sport publishers.

### Tier 4 — Audience-signal sources
Public social reaction, creator conversation and community responses. These show reception, not effectiveness proof.

## Source rules

- Facts should be confirmed through primary or authoritative sources wherever possible.
- Reported commercial outcomes must not be inferred from social reaction.
- Interpretations must be labelled as Monokromatik analysis.
- Every intelligence record requires stored citations and source dates.

## Visual rights rules

| Asset source | Default use |
|---|---|
| Owned creative or photography | Publish |
| Licensed stock imagery | Publish under licence record |
| Official brand media/press kit | Use only with retained usage basis and attribution |
| Official video/social embed | Embed where permitted |
| Publisher-owned editorial image | Link or request permission; do not default-hotlink |
| Unknown ownership | Do not publish |

## Required architectural change

The existing automated image-sourcing behaviour that scrapes and hotlinks source publisher images must be replaced for Monokromatik 2.0 with a rights-status record and safe-asset workflow.
