# Source, Media Use and Attribution Policy

## Editorial purpose

Monokromatik is a visually led editorial and intelligence platform. Campaigns, films, visual identity, products, experiences, creators, places and people cannot be analysed meaningfully through text alone.

The operating standard is therefore not visual timidity. It is **bold, attributable and proportionate editorial use**:

> Source widely. Credit clearly. Use media in service of original analysis. Avoid treating someone else's work as unowned inventory.

## Source hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Primary evidence
Official brand, agency, campaign, athlete, creator, event, company, rights-holder, sport-property or institutional materials.

Use for campaign facts, assets, creative credits, statements, launches, official films and reported results.

### Tier 2 — Authoritative professional intelligence
Reputable marketing, advertising, creative, research, business, awards and effectiveness platforms.

Examples of intended monitoring categories include WARC, Cannes Lions, Effie, Campaign, The Drum, Adweek, Ad Age, Contagious, Creative Review, Loeries, MarkLives, Bizcommunity, African Marketing Confederation and relevant research/business publications, subject to access and usage terms.

Use for discovery, professional context, benchmarks and attributable evidence.

### Tier 3 — Cultural and local context
Credible African and diaspora culture, music, entertainment, fashion, hospitality and sport publishers.

Use to understand cultural movements, local interpretation, emerging voices and audience context.

### Tier 4 — Audience and social signals
Public social posts, creator conversation, comments and public reaction.

Use as indicators of reception or conversation only. These signals do not independently prove business or effectiveness outcomes.

## Source rules

- Facts should be confirmed through primary or authoritative sources wherever practical.
- Reported commercial or effectiveness outcomes must be stated only where supported by credible evidence.
- Cultural response may be analysed, but must not be presented as proven commercial impact without evidence.
- Interpretation should be recognisable as Monokromatik analysis.
- Intelligence records should retain source names, links, dates and evidence confidence.
- Paid, gated or licensed intelligence sources may inform commissioned work only through permitted access and appropriate citation; they are not to be indiscriminately scraped or republished.

## Media-use framework

Monokromatik should pursue a rich editorial visual standard: campaign stills, official films, credited product imagery, portraits, photography, designed report covers, event imagery and motion-led hero experiences.

| Classification | Typical material | Default site action |
|---|---|---|
| **Owned / commissioned** | Monokromatik photography, video, illustration, data design or commissioned portraiture | Publish prominently; use for covers and premium hero placements |
| **Licensed** | Licensed stock, licensed photography or partner-provided rights-cleared assets | Publish within licence terms; store licence/source record |
| **Official embed** | Official brand, agency, creator, event, YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram or approved platform-hosted films/posts | Embed prominently with visible credit and source link where platform terms allow |
| **Official campaign / publicity asset** | Brand newsroom images, campaign stills, agency case-study assets, official press assets | Use proportionately in editorial commentary/case studies with attribution and retained source record |
| **Award / case-study showcase asset** | Official award-entry video or images and creative-platform case-study presentation | Use or embed for analysis with source credit, observing platform terms and access conditions |
| **Editorial reference asset** | Publisher-owned photography or third-party reporting visuals | Prefer link, embed or credited limited reference use where editorially justified; avoid making it default cover inventory |
| **Public social signal** | Creator or audience social post material | Embed or cite where relevant to reception analysis; avoid unauthorised rehosting |
| **Unclear origin / disputed** | Images or video without traceable source or uncertain rights context | Replace, hold or obtain permission before prominent use |

## Hero and video strategy

To deliver a high-impact Living Magazine experience, the platform may use:

- officially embedded campaign films and brand/agency case-study videos;
- rights-cleared or official campaign stills for relevant analysis;
- credited press/publicity imagery;
- commissioned or owned visual cover treatments;
- motion graphics and designed report-cover treatments;
- AI-assisted editorial artwork only where clearly framed as illustration and not misrepresented as documentary evidence.

For premium cover placements, rights-cleared, official, embedded or commissioned media should be prioritised over scraped publisher imagery.

## Attribution standard

Where external media materially contributes to a story or case study, the published experience should display or store:

- creator/brand/agency/publisher credit, as applicable;
- original source link or official embed;
- campaign or publication date where available;
- usage classification;
- any known restrictions or permissions.

## Automation rules

AI agents may:

- discover potential imagery and video;
- identify likely official campaign assets or embed URLs;
- suggest visual treatments;
- capture attribution fields;
- flag uncertain origin, missing credits or rights risk;
- recommend alternate official/owned assets.

AI agents may not:

- strip attribution;
- claim rights ownership for external assets;
- automatically rehost large volumes of publisher-owned imagery as cover inventory;
- use unclear media prominently after a rights-risk flag;
- circumvent gated access or source restrictions.

## Transition from the existing system

The current platform contains externally hosted imagery inherited from its early editorial workflow. Monokromatik 2.0 will move toward a media record model that classifies visuals and supports richer official, embedded, licensed and owned media use.

This transition should not make the site visually timid. It should make visual ambition sustainable, attributable and credible to the brands, agencies, creators and audiences the platform serves.
