# Official Media Enrichment Workflow

## Objective

Evolve Monokromatik articles from text-and-thumbnail publishing into rich, attributable editorial experiences using official and authority-sourced image, film, audio, graphic and document materials.

This applies to Issue 001 and to the broader article archive over time.

## Editorial principle

A richer platform does not require weaker source discipline.

> The reader should experience the work through its most credible source material, while clearly understanding what belongs to the campaign authority, what comes from independent reporting and what is Monokromatik interpretation.

## Eligible media inputs

| Source category | Suitable usage | Default treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Official brand newsroom | Campaign stills, product assets, films, press releases, executive statements | Display or embed with visible official-source credit |
| Official agency/campaign page | Case-film embeds, creative credits, production details and rationale | Embed or link with agency/campaign credit |
| Official creator/rightsholder channel | Creator-authorised stills, audio/video, social embeds and credits | Embed or use as explicitly permitted; name the creator/rightsholder |
| Award/effectiveness platform | Award film, case summary, credited effectiveness evidence | Link/embed where terms allow; attribute platform and entry |
| Reputable publisher/reporting source | Reporting, external context, critical reception and reported outcomes | Cite for evidence; do not default to using its photography as page inventory |
| Monokromatik-owned design | Cover artwork, explainers, diagrams, data graphics and social cards | Use freely with Monokromatik credit |

## Per-story enrichment workflow

### Stage 1 — Story audit

For every existing or new article, capture:

- the brand, campaign, person, event or cultural property being covered;
- whether the story contains a claim requiring verification;
- whether official brand/agency/creator media likely exists;
- whether the story belongs in Signal, Intelligence, Culture or an Issue;
- whether it deserves premium media treatment or a lighter linked-source treatment.

### Stage 2 — Authority discovery

Search for media in this order:

1. Official brand newsroom or press centre.
2. Official agency case-study or campaign page.
3. Official creator, partner, rightsholder or event property.
4. Official platform-hosted video/audio post suitable for embed.
5. Award/effectiveness entry where permitted.
6. Independent publisher content for reporting context only, unless explicit media-use basis is recorded.

### Stage 3 — Asset classification

Every potential asset must be classified before display:

- `owned`
- `official_brand_media`
- `official_agency_media`
- `official_embed`
- `licensed`
- `permissioned_contributor_media`
- `editorial_reference_only`
- `hold_unclear_rights`

### Stage 4 — Enrichment design

Use media deliberately, not decoratively:

| Article type | Recommended media structure |
|---|---|
| The Work case study | Hero campaign still or official film, 2–4 credited execution/design assets, evidence/source rail |
| Will It Land? | Original official campaign film or visual, local-context graphic/voice response, verdict panel |
| Brand Weather | Modular source cards, official thumbnails where suitable, signal-confidence and watchlist treatment |
| Conversations | Contributor portrait or recording, approved pull quotes, audio/video cut and transcript excerpt |
| Cover essay | Monokromatik-owned cover world plus selected officially credited case references |
| Culture dispatch | Credited visual material from official creator/property source or clearly permissioned editorial use |

### Stage 5 — Credit and source record

Each visible external asset requires:

- owner/source-authority name;
- visible credit line;
- original source URL;
- intended editorial usage classification;
- verification date;
- status if permission, licensing or embed availability changes.

### Stage 6 — Publication review

Before publication, confirm:

- image/video/audio renders in preview and production-ready environment;
- all credits appear correctly and remain legible on mobile;
- official-media assets remain accessible;
- no publisher-owned asset has been repurposed without usage basis;
- claims supported by a source are not overstated;
- Monokromatik opinion is visibly distinguished from reported fact;
- contributors have approved quotations and supplied/recorded media.

## Archive enrichment priority model

Not every existing article requires full treatment at once. Prioritise as follows:

| Priority | Story type | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issue 001 launch features | Complete official-media and source validation before launch |
| 2 | High-value Signal and Intelligence stories | Convert into richer case-study or analysis formats |
| 3 | Articles with major brands, campaigns or creator partnerships | Add official source cards and media where available |
| 4 | Cultural dispatches with strong owned/official visual potential | Upgrade selectively for browsing depth |
| 5 | Low-value or dated posts | Archive, consolidate or leave in lighter format pending relevance review |

## Audio strategy

Audio should be introduced where there is an official or commissioned source, not manufactured as filler:

- official campaign sound or podcast/audio embeds where attributable and permitted;
- recorded contributor conversations with permission;
- Monokromatik audio essays or narrated briefings produced as owned content;
- no ripping or repackaging of third-party audio without permission.

No standalone official audio asset has yet been identified for the Nike × Air Afrique or Orange features. Their current release package is therefore visual/video-led.

## Technical implications for Track 2

The future content model should support:

- media records linked to stories and case studies;
- asset-type rendering components for image, video, audio, embed, document and designed graphic;
- source authority and rights-state fields;
- automated discovery suggestions with human media approval;
- broken-asset checks and replacement workflow;
- source-led image/video gallery population for new features;
- archive enrichment queues ranked by editorial value.
