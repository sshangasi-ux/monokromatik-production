This file is the **method of record** for the legal and structural layer of a culture-driven
transaction — the part that decides whether authorship can actually be *owned*. Where a legal
partner delivers the formal opinion, we bring the map of what matters and why, informed by the
value-capture read.

## The skill areas (what "good" looks like)

1. **IP & rights ownership — the core question.** Who actually owns the thing that certifies
   the value: trademarks, copyrights, masters vs publishing, image/name rights, broadcast &
   licensing rights, catalogues, brand IP. Chain of title, encumbrances, reversion, and the
   *gap between who authored and who holds the paper* — the legal face of the AOC read.
2. **Commercial-contract DD** — the licences, distribution, sponsorship, platform and agency
   agreements where value quietly leaks: exclusivity, term, territory, change-of-control,
   revenue-share and sell-on clauses, and who can walk with the audience/data.
3. **Corporate & ownership structure** — cap table, control vs economics, minority protections,
   holdco/jurisdiction (and the HOLLOWED pattern: a local licence over an offshore-owned
   platform — legally real, economically empty).
4. **Regulatory & data** — sector licences (betting, fintech, broadcast), data-protection
   exposure (POPIA/NDPA/GDPR), local-ownership/FDI rules (B-BBEE and equivalents), and
   merger-control thresholds.
5. **Reputational & integrity (legal-adjacent)** — litigation, sanctions/PEP, disputes over
   authorship or rights, and cultural-legitimacy risk that no data room lists.
6. **Structuring the fix** — the legal instruments that move an asset up the ladder: sell-on
   and image-rights vehicles, licensing and JV structures, IP-holding entities, and
   direct-to-fan/data ownership — so the post-deal retention plan is executable.
