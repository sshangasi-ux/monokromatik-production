# The Boardroom / The Backroom — Founding Contributor Target Plan

## Founding conversation proposition

**Working title:** What Global Brands Still Get Wrong About African Relevance  
**Issue:** Monokromatik Issue 001 — *The Intelligence Behind African Influence*  
**Format:** Three-perspective conversation, edited long-form feature, optional film/audio cut and approved quote package.

## Why these three voices

The first conversation needs three different forms of authority rather than three people saying the same thing:

1. A senior brand/platform leader who understands power, investment and institutional decision-making.
2. A cultural-commercial author who can speak to African creativity as owned value, not borrowed aesthetic.
3. A South African women’s-sport authority who can challenge the **Will It Land?** relevance test from lived, high-performance context.

## Recommended interview architecture

### Shared opening question

> African influence is visible across global brand culture. Where do brands still confuse visibility with genuine relevance or value creation?

### Individual question lanes

#### Nunu Ntshingila-Njeke — Brand / platform lens

- What structural decision tells you a global brand is serious about Africa rather than simply borrowing relevance?
- Where do global measurement systems fail to see African value clearly?
- What should a global CMO be investing in before the opportunity becomes obvious to everyone?

#### Thebe Magugu — Cultural authorship / premium commerce lens

- What changes when African heritage and place are treated as the source of premium value rather than campaign texture?
- What makes collaboration feel authored, equitable and enduring?
- How should brands behave around communities, craft and cultural memory they did not create?

#### Desiree Ellis — Sport / visibility lens

- What does recognition mean after a team has already proved excellence on the field?
- What would meaningful brand support for South African women’s football look like beyond a strong film or message?
- What must change so that attention becomes an ongoing system rather than a tournament moment?

## Outreach controls

Before any invitation is sent or any contributor appears publicly:

- verify current role and preferred contact route;
- state clearly that this is an editorial commission, not paid endorsement unless separately agreed and disclosed;
- offer intended format, time commitment and quote-approval process;
- record any commercial relationships or conflicts;
- secure rights for portraits, film, audio or supplied assets;
- do not publish quotations or imply participation before written agreement.
