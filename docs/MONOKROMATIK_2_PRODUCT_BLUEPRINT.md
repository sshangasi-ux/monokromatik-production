# Monokromatik 2.0 Product Blueprint

## Approved proposition

**MONOKROMATIK**  
**African Culture. Global Influence. Brand Intelligence.**

Monokromatik is an AI-enabled editorial and intelligence network decoding the campaigns, creators, athletes, brands and cultural movements shaping Africa and its diaspora.

## Strategic ambition

Build the authoritative African culture and brand-intelligence network that:
- discovers relevant cultural and commercial signals early;
- analyses brand and campaign activity with African and diaspora fluency;
- produces original editorial work and structured intelligence;
- supports marketers, creators, founders, agencies, partners and investors;
- evolves into a searchable intelligence and research product.

## Technical direction

Retain:
- Next.js / Vercel frontend foundation
- GitHub source control
- Kit newsletter integration
- Claude capability

Introduce:
- OpenAI as a first-class provider and automatic operational fallback for Claude tasks;
- Supabase/Postgres for structured intelligence, workflow state, source and rights records;
- formal editorial review queues;
- rights-controlled visual asset handling;
- grounded reader intelligence assistant.

## Autonomy rule

Monokromatik should automate recurring operational work and learn from verified outcomes with minimal routine human intervention.

Human approval remains mandatory for:
- Campaign Decoded and Would It Travel? publication;
- paid intelligence and reports;
- reputational/commercial performance claims;
- sponsored content;
- corrections and disputes;
- uncertain image-rights decisions.
