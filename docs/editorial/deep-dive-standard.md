# The deep-dive standard — unpack, don't conclude

The default for a MonoKromatik **feature deep dive** (a free long-form article, not a
paid report). Reference builds: `liv-golf-bankruptcy-rebel-league-vs-the-legacy-it-tried-to-break`
and `nba-europe-vs-euroleague-the-incumbent-as-insurgent`.

## The register: interrogative, not positional
A deep dive **unpacks a deal or a moment and lays out what could — and couldn't —
come next.** It does not establish a verdict. The reader should leave understanding
the mechanics, the tensions and the full range of plausible outcomes, and trusting
that we didn't decide the ending in advance.

- **Interrogate the deal.** Get inside the actual mechanics — the numbers, the cap
  table, the governance, the legal fight, the incentives. Specifics over vibes.
- **Hold both branches open.** Where the story forks, write *both* the optimistic and
  the pessimistic scenario, in good faith, and leave them in tension. "It could work;
  it could also be the epilogue" is a legitimate landing.
- **The lens, not the verdict.** The MonoKromatik value-capture / ownership angle is a
  *lens* we apply, explicitly flagged as such — "that is not an accusation, it is a
  lens" — never the conclusion the piece is bent toward.
- **No strawmen.** The strongest version of each side. If we can't argue the other
  branch convincingly, we haven't understood the deal yet.

## The structural bar
- **Length:** ~1,200–1,800 words. (A deep dive is not a brief; a brief stays short.)
- **Structure:** 6–8 named `## ` sections. A workable spine: the hook / the question ·
  the bet or the deal · the mechanics that matter · the pushback or the counter-force ·
  **what could flourish** · **what could fail** · the African/diaspora lens · an open
  close.
- **Evidence:** 8–12 named, credible sources; inline `[n]` citations that map exactly to
  `sources[]`; a `numbers` module of the deal's key figures; figures traceable.
- **The African thread:** find the genuine African/diaspora value-capture angle and run
  it as one lens among several — never forced, never the whole verdict. If there is no
  honest African thread, the topic may not belong on this desk.
- **Media:** a real, sourced, topical hero image (see `MEDIA-STANDARD.md`) — or none.
  Never fabricated or generic.

## The brandRead
- `confidence: "interpretive"` (an exploratory read, not a verified claim of fact).
- `take`: an honest statement of the tension and the open question — what is settled,
  what genuinely isn't — and an explicit refusal to pick the ending early.
- `pullQuote`: a **question**, not a slogan.

## The free / paid line
This standard is for **free feature deep dives**. A **paid report** is different: it may
take a considered position, and it must carry the Bear Case module and the segmented
so-what — see [`paid-deliverable-standard.md`](./paid-deliverable-standard.md). A deep
dive persuades by unpacking; a report persuades by concluding well and arguing against
itself.
