# The signalling lens — why the mispricing holds

A complementary lens to **Authorship → Ownership → Capture (AOC)**. AOC maps *where*
value leaks; the signalling lens explains *why* the leak is structural rather than
temporary — and sharpens the strategic move. Reference build: `data/reports.json` →
`whos-buying-african-sport-2026`, §04 **"THE SIGNAL READ"** (the first worked
application; this doc is abstracted from it).

Provenance: Rory Sutherland, *Hacking the Unconscious* (BBC Radio 4 / BBC Audio,
2017–2018; Audible ed. 2021), building on Michael Spence's economics of market
signalling and Amotz Zahavi's handicap principle (and, further back, Veblen).

## The idea in one paragraph
The value of an asset or an action frequently lies not in its function but in **what it
credibly signals** — and the only signals worth anything are the ones **too costly to
fake**. A diamond's worth is mostly its expensiveness-as-message (Sutherland's De Beers
case); a peacock's tail is credible *because* it is a handicap (Zahavi); a degree
signals more than it teaches (Spence). The test of a real signal is always the same:
could a pretender cheaply counterfeit it? If yes, it carries no information and no
premium. If no, the inimitability *is* the value.

## The MonoKromatik move: certification sits between authorship and capture
This is our own contribution, and it's what makes the lens more than a borrowed idea.
Signalling theory says the premium accrues **to whoever wears and certifies the signal,
not to whoever produces it.** So we insert a middle term into AOC:

> **Authorship → *Certification* → Capture.**

The **certification market** is the high-status venue where a hard-to-fake signal is
validated, graded and *priced*. A European league is the certification market for
African talent — which is exactly why a player's value "crystallises" abroad. Capture
happens where certification happens. When the producer of a signal doesn't own a
high-status market to certify it, the signal must travel to be priced, and the premium
lands wherever it's certified. That is the signalling statement of the AOC capture gap —
and the reason it's **self-sustaining**: an under-certified asset reads as low-status,
which depresses its price, which starves the domestic apparatus that could certify it.

**The strategic sharpening:** "own the pipe / own the rights" becomes **own the
status-conferring apparatus** — the certification market itself, not only the production
behind it.

## How to apply it (the procedure)
For any asset, deal or moment on the desk, run five questions:
1. **Name the signal.** What is actually being signalled — status, authenticity,
   belonging, competence, taste?
2. **Test hard-to-fake.** Could a pretender counterfeit it cheaply? If yes, there is no
   premium to analyse. If no, that inimitability is the value — say why.
3. **Locate the certification market.** Where does the signal get validated and priced —
   and who owns that venue?
4. **Follow the premium.** Who *wears* the signal in the market where it's priced, and
   therefore captures the premium — versus who merely produced it?
5. **Derive the move.** What would it take to own (or build) the certification apparatus,
   so the premium is captured where the signal is authored?

## The guardrail: it must generate a testable claim
Signalling theory is seductive because it can "explain" anything — which is its failure
mode. **If everything is signalling, the lens explains nothing.** Every application must
end in a claim that could be checked or falsified, not a rhetorical flourish. In the
flagship the claim is: *the capture premium tracks proximity to the certification
market, not proximity to production.* That is a statement someone could test and argue
with. If your signalling read doesn't produce one, you're decorating, not analysing —
cut it. The lens is a claim-generator, never a substitute for the numbers.

## Worked examples (from the flagship)
- **Talent.** The player is a hard-to-fake signal of footballing quality; the European
  league is the certification market; the transfer/wage premium is captured there, not
  at the Dakar academy. → Own equity in the pathway and the image-rights vehicle that
  certifies the *authenticity* premium at home.
- **Cultural authenticity.** A global brand buying AFCON or an African athlete's
  endorsement is renting an authenticity it cannot manufacture internally. → Sell it as
  a *premium* signal to the diaspora and global-neutral, not clear it at a discount.
- **Fandom (betting).** The attention is a genuine signal at continental scale, but it's
  certified and monetised on foreign-owned platforms — HOLLOWED in AOC terms, uncertified
  at home in signalling terms. → The certification apparatus (and the data behind it) is
  the asset to repatriate.
