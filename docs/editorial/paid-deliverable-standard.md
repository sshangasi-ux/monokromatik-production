# The paid-deliverable standard — MonoKromatik

Anything a reader pays for — reports, brand studies, brand proposals, strategic
documents, commissioned case studies — must clear this bar. If it doesn't, it
ships free or it doesn't ship. A paywall on thin work costs more brand equity
than it earns in revenue.

## The register: consultancy rigour × culture-agency fluency
Every paid piece is a fusion of two houses:

- **The strategy house (Bain / BCG / McKinsey):** a defensible framework, a
  bottom-up evidence base, a sector/segment read, an explicit "so what" broken
  out by audience, a stated methodology, and full provenance. The reader should
  be able to lift a chart into a boardroom deck and defend it.
- **The culture house (Translation / SpringHill / The Robot Company):** culture
  is treated as an ownable asset, not decoration. We read authorship vs.
  ownership, narrative leverage, and brand-through-culture with the fluency of
  operators who build value *from* culture — not analysts who observe it. The
  cultural read is a section, not a garnish.

The combination is the moat: no pure consultancy has the cultural authority, and
no culture shop has the sourced-data rigour. We hold both.

## The structural bar (a paid piece)
- **Length:** 2,500–3,500 words. (Below ~2,000 it is a brief, and briefs are free.)
- **Structure:** 7–8 named sections — typically: the headline/finding · the
  framework · the evidence/ledger · the sector or segment read · the
  counter-case (exceptions / bear case) · the cultural read · the segmented
  "what this means for you" · methodology & sources.
- **Evidence:** 8–15 named, credible sources; an original data exhibit
  (chart/scorecard) whose note names its own sources; figures labelled
  "reported" vs "verified".
- **The so-what:** recommendations broken out by audience (founders / capital /
  policymakers / brands & agencies) — never a single generic conclusion.
- **Provenance is the product:** every claim traceable, cross-linked to the
  tracker / Index / explainer it rests on. It must be citable, presentable and
  defensible.

## Agentic & orchestrated production
These are not hot takes; they are synthesised. The production method is
orchestrative: pull the structured data (the tracker, the Index, sources),
verify the figures against named reporting, assemble the framework, write the
cultural read, and derive the segmented recommendations from the evidence — so
the finished document reads as the output of a rigorous intelligence process,
not an opinion. Reference build: the Value-Capture Scorecard
(`data/reports.json` → value-capture-scorecard-2026), rebuilt to this standard.

## The free / paid line

- **Paid:** the deep reports (this standard) · the full Index dataset / licence ·
